About

1. This app shows 140k global earthquakes with 2.5+ magnitude from 2020 - 2024

2. The hoverable pie chart shows earthquake magnitude distribution

3. The brushable histogram shows the number of quakes over time, grouped by month

4. Change map skins and scale to your preference

5. Switch between magnitude mode and depth mode
